# Couch co-op sound: every local player's sound effects are heard, the music
# only from player 1's world. P2 goes into Link's house (a world of its own)
# and swings the sword while P1 stands still outside. With "Everyone's sound
# effects" on, the extra driver of P2's world sounds (the swings); off, it
# doesn't. The main driver (P1's world, with the music) renders exactly the
# same either way, and so does the game (the state hash): the extra drivers
# are outside the game's state. P1's music keeps playing although P2's world
# loaded the dungeon songs (only the heard world's songs go to the driver),
# and when P1 follows P2 inside, the house's song plays.
load 2
run 30
join 2
run 10
clearsprites
pos 2 3920 1690
pram 2 2f 00
hold 2 U
run 300
hold 2 -
expect_apart 1 2
expect_instances 2
view 2
expect_module 7
view 1
coopsfx 1
audioreset
hold 2 B
audiorun 4
hold 2 -
audiorun 40
hold 2 B
audiorun 4
hold 2 -
audiorun 40
hold 2 B
audiorun 4
hold 2 -
audiorun 40
hold 2 B
audiorun 4
hold 2 -
audiorun 100
expect_audio main 500 32767
expect_audio sfx 50 32767
audiomark swings
hashmark swings
