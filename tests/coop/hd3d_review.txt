# 3D view, first person: the view starts where it should. Switching to
# first person while walking keeps the Link walking the same way (the d-pad
# is not turned under a held direction until it is let go), also when the
# 3D view comes back on during the walk, when first person is entered again
# during it, and with "Controls turn with camera" off. Turning the 3D view
# off and on again in first person looks the way the Link now faces.
# Straight stairs to another floor turn the view to where the Link comes
# out facing. Where the picture is 2D the d-pad isn't turned, also through
# the game's scrolls. A check (expect_exact3d) leaves the camera alone.
load 2
run 30
view3d 1
drawtick 1
tick 3
clearsprites
# Walking south, then a left click: still walking south.
hold 1 D
tick 20
markpos 1
click left
tick 30
expect_fp 1 1
expect_cam3d 1 180 8
expect_moved 1 south
# Let go, then down again: now the d-pad turns with the view, and down
# walks back from where it looks (north).
hold 1 -
tick 2
hold 1 D
markpos 1
tick 20
expect_moved 1 north
hold 1 -
tick 2
# Off, a walk south in 2D, on again: first person looks south.
cam3d 1 fp 90 8
tick 3
view3d 0
hold 1 D
tick 20
hold 1 -
tick 2
view3d 1
tick 3
expect_fp 1 1
expect_3d 1
expect_cam3d 1 180 8
