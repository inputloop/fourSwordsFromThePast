# 3D view: each player on one computer chooses it for themselves (the menu;
# F11 is player 1's). Two players in one world: both in 3D or both in 2D
# share a pane; one in 2D and one in 3D get a pane each, and only the 3D
# player's pane is drawn in 3D. A 2D player's d-pad is never turned, their
# View button and stick do nothing, and their choice of first person waits
# until they are back in 3D. Turning the view off for everybody leaves one
# pane per world, as always.
load 2
run 30
localjoin 2
tick 30
expect_players 2
pos 1 3860 1660
pos 2 3860 1700
tick 3
# Both 3D: one pane, drawn in 3D.
view3d 1
expect_panes 1
expect_3d 1
# Player 2 picks 2D: two panes; player 1's is 3D, player 2's is 2D.
view3d 0 2
expect_panes 2
view 1
expect_3d 1
view 2
expect_3d 0 off
view 1
splitshot tests/out/hd3d_perplayer_split.ppm 1 2
# Player 1's camera turned east turns only player 1's d-pad: player 2's up
# still walks north.
cam3d 1 tp 90 45
drawrun 2
expect_remap 1 L U
expect_remap 2 U U
expect_remap 2 L L
hold 2 U
markpos 2
drawrun 20
expect_moved 2 north
hold 2 -
drawrun 2
# Player 2's View button and stick do nothing in 2D.
viewbutton 2
expect_fp 2 0
expect_panes 2
stick3d 2 100 0
cam3dstep 300
stick3d 2 0 0
expect_cam3d 1 90 45 1
expect_cam3d 2 0 45 0
# The other way round: player 1 in 2D, player 2 in 3D and in first person.
cam3d 1 tp 0 45
view3d 0 1
view3d 1 2
expect_panes 2
view 1
expect_3d 0 off
view 2
expect_3d 1
view 1
viewbutton 2
expect_fp 2 1
expect_panes 2
expect_remap 1 U U
# Player 1's F11 is player 1's alone: 3D on again, two panes (player 2 in
# first person); player 2 back in third person: one shared pane.
key3d toggle
expect_panes 2
expect_3d 1
viewbutton 2
expect_fp 2 0
expect_panes 1
# Both 2D: one pane per world, as always.
view3d 0
expect_panes 1
expect_3d 0 off
# Nothing of it changes the game.
view3d 1
view3d 0 2
expect_drawpure 3
