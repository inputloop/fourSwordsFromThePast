# 3D view: the game plays exactly the same with it on. The same walk is
# played three times from the same snapshot: with the 3D view off, on in
# third person at the classic angle, and on in first person looking north
# (where the d-pad isn't turned); the game's state hash must come out the
# same each time. Outdoors, through the Sanctuary's door into the next room
# (the 3D view keeps the old room while the new one loads) and in a dark cave.
#
# Outdoors.
load 2
drawrun 30
clearsprites
pos 1 3860 1690
mark
view3d 0
hold 1 R
drawrun 120
hold 1 D
drawrun 60
hold 1 -
hashmark out
rewind
view3d 1
cam3d 1 tp 0
hold 1 R
drawrun 120
hold 1 D
drawrun 60
hold 1 -
expect_hashmark out
rewind
cam3d 1 fp 0
hold 1 R
drawrun 120
hold 1 D
drawrun 60
hold 1 -
expect_hashmark out
