# "Watch everyone" online (run together with net_multiview_client.txt): the
# client's player (P2) starts in a world of its own (at its house) and walks
# around there. Both computers watch everyone and draw every pane each tick,
# as the game does: the host gets a 2D pane for P2 (who plays on the other
# computer), the client one for P1. Only the picture changes: the computers
# stay in sync (no resync), rollback corrections just show in the panes.

drawtick 1

tick 480
