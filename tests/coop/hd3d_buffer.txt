# 3D view: drawing never writes outside the picture it was given. Each
# frame is drawn into the middle of a buffer three times as wide (and two
# rows higher and lower) filled with a pattern, in the two phases split
# screen uses, then the overlay on top; no pattern pixel may change. At
# every widescreen width, with a video chip that still has an old width, with
# the camera turned and in first person, on the world map (4x), through a
# door, in the Sanctuary and in a dark cave.
view3d 1
load 2
drawrun 120
drawguard 60
widescreen 71
drawrun 30
drawguard 60
widescreen 96
drawrun 30
drawguard 60
# The video chip still set for 40 columns a side (drawing must use the setting).
stalewidth 1 40
drawguard 30
widescreen 0
stalewidth 1 96
drawguard 30
widescreen 71
# Turned, then in first person.
cam3d 1 tp -60 30
drawguard 60
cam3d 1 tp 120 75 200
drawguard 30
cam3d 1 fp
drawguard 60
cam3d 1 fp 200 -60
drawguard 30
cam3d 1 tp 0 45
# The world map (X), drawn at 4x, and back.
hold 1 X
run 2
hold 1 -
drawguard 90
expect_module 14
hold 1 X
run 2
hold 1 -
drawguard 90

drawguard 30
cam3d 1 tp 0 45
