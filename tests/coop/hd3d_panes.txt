# 3D view: split screen. Two players on one computer in one world share a
# pane (and its camera) in third person. A player who switches to first
# person gets a pane of their own; switching back merges the panes again.
# Every stick in a shared pane turns that pane's camera. A downed player
# watching a teammate sees the teammate in third person (no pane of its own)
# and keeps its first-person choice for later.
load 2
run 30
localjoin 2
tick 30
expect_players 2
expect_panes 1
view3d 1
expect_panes 1
expect_3d 1
viewbutton 2
expect_fp 2 1
expect_panes 2
# Split screen draws in two phases (the worlds read, then the 3D panes).
splitshot tests/out/hd3d_panes_split.ppm 1 2
viewbutton 2
expect_fp 2 0
expect_panes 1
# The View button held: back to the classic view.
cam3d 1 tp 40 50
viewbutton 1 600
expect_cam3d 1 0 45 0
# A shared pane: player 2's stick turns player 1's camera (the pane's).
stick3d 2 100 0
cam3dstep 300
stick3d 2 0 0
cam3dstep 300
expect_cam3d 1 45 45 10
expect_cam3d 2 0 45 0
viewbutton 1 600
# Both in first person: two panes; one of them back in third person: still
# two; both back: one.
viewbutton 1
viewbutton 2
expect_fp 1 1
expect_fp 2 1
expect_panes 2
viewbutton 1
expect_panes 2
viewbutton 2
expect_fp 1 0
expect_fp 2 0
expect_panes 1
# Player 2 down, watching player 1: no first-person pane, the choice kept.
viewbutton 2
expect_panes 2
down 2
tick 2
hold 2 r
tick 2
hold 2 -
tick 2
expect_fp 2 1
expect_panes 1
revive 2
tick 2
expect_panes 2
# With the 3D view off: one pane per world, as always.
view3d 0
expect_panes 1
