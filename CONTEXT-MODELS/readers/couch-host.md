# I am the one who set it up for everyone

*Layer 2 · a reader of the player's book, in the first person. A role.*

It is my computer, my controllers, my evening. Three friends are on the sofa and one of them is
already pressing buttons. I need to know how they join, why one of them is a ghost, and what to tell
the friend who is at home and wants in.

**What I need from a section:** one screen of reading, then back to the game. A section I can open
from the menu while everyone waits (that is the help key's whole reason, §16).

**What would make me close the book:** having to read about online play to learn about the couch.
They are separate routes, §7 and §9, and each says so.
