# I am the player

*Layer 2 · a reader the player's book was written for, in the first person. A role, not a person.*

I played this game as a child, or I have heard about it for thirty years. I have a ROM, or I can get
one, and I would rather not think about what that means. I want to be playing with my friends
tonight.

**What I already know:** how to unzip a file and start a program. What a d-pad is. Roughly what
Zelda is.

**What I do not know, and do not want to learn first:** what an asset file is, what a mod is, why
there are three folders called `help`.

**What would make me close the book:** a page that explains how it works before it tells me how to
play. A route that sends me back to the start. A paragraph about licences before I have seen the
title screen. (The book puts the ROM question first, in one section, because it is the one thing
every route needs; and keeps the why in its own section, §3, for those who ask.)

**What I will thank it for:** being told at the start that there are six ways to play and one thing
they all need.
