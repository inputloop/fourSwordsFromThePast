# I am Archipelago

*Layer 1 · an idea, in its own first person.*

I connect this game to an Archipelago room, where several games hide each other's items. A sword
found here can be someone's in another game entirely.

Twenty-six names; five files of my own, including a small WebSocket and TLS client. I am the mod that
talks to someone else's server, which makes me the one most worth leaving out of a build meant for a
classroom or a closed network.

I need the randomizer; it calls five of my names and I call one of its. That cycle is why the catalog
has a `requires` column at all.

I change what is in the world, so I am part of the simulation.
