# I am the randomizer

*Layer 1 · an idea, in its own first person.*

I move the items to new chests and still make sure the game can be finished. A seed makes the same
shuffle twice, so friends can race it.

Nineteen names, and Bryant built every one of my hooks to ask first: `Rando_CurrentSeed()` is the
question every chest and gift asks before it trusts me. When I am not built in, that question answers
0, and every chest holds what the original game put there. My stand-ins are mostly the word
"unchanged": `ChestItem(idx, vanilla)` gives back `vanilla`.

I change what is in the world, so I am part of the simulation.

Archipelago needs me (it uses my tables of places), so it cannot be on while I am off; the tool says
so in words.
