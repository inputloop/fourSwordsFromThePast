# I am the network

*Layer 1 · an idea, in its own first person.*

Sockets, room codes, a router asked to open a port. I am what lets four people in four houses be in
one game.

I am smaller than people expect. The session code that keeps everyone in step (rollback, the frame
counter, who is joined) also runs a couch game, so it is not mine; it stays. What is mine is the
network itself: seventeen names, almost all called only by the session code. Without me the session
cannot open a socket, says so in its own words ("Unable to open the network port"), and the couch
plays on.

I am marked as part of the simulation, not because a socket changes the game, but because a copy
without me cannot be in a game with one, and the version on the wire should say what a copy is.
