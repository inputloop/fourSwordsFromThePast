# I am the story

*Layer 1 · an idea, in its own first person.*

I am the retold legend: a blade that split one knight into four, in green, red, blue and purple. I
replace a handful of the game's own lines after the title screen, and I put "Four Swords of the
Past" over the original.

I am cheap. Four names, all small: the game asks me whether I have a message for a slot, and I
either hand one back or say no. With me left out the program is 28,928 bytes smaller and the original
legend plays, untouched.

I change what the game *says*, and online the host's choice is used, so I am marked as changing the
simulation: two copies that disagree about me should not play together, and the wire version says so.

**Who wants me:** anyone playing with three friends, who wants the opening to explain why there are
four of them. **Who does not:** a purist, and a translator, since I am written in English only.

**My tests:** `story` and `story_text` fail without me; `story_off` passes without me (it tests the
game with me switched off at run time, which is the same picture). The catalog tagged all three; the
matrix says two.
