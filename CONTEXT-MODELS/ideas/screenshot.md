# I am the screenshot key

*Layer 1 · an idea, in its own first person.*

F12, and a PNG lands in `screenshots/`. Ctrl+F12, and the same picture without the mod's labels.

Five names, used only by the main loop and the tests. I change nothing in the game, so I am not part
of the simulation; two copies that differ about me play together happily. Without me the program
is 27,960 bytes smaller.

I am the smallest honest example of a mod that touches the main loop, which is why I am a good first
one to switch off when you are learning how switching works.

**My test:** `screenshot` fails without me, as it should, and nothing else does.
