# I am co-op

*Layer 1 · an idea, in its own first person. Numbers from `../data/census.tsv` and the commands
named there.*

I am the reason this is not just zelda3. Four Links, one world or several, hearts in every corner.

People will ask why I am not a switch like the others. Here is my case, in numbers. The files that
are me (`mp.c`, `instance.c`, and the session code that runs a couch game as well as an online one)
define 138 names that the rest of the program calls, from 22 other files (in a build with every mod on, the help key included). The game's own Link code,
its enemies, its doors and its camera all ask me questions now, every frame. An "off" for me would
not be a stand-in; it would be a second game, kept alive beside the first, forever.

So I am not a mod. I am the game, as Bryant ships it. What *can* be switched is everything that
leans on me: the looks, the shuffles, the network.

**What would change my mind:** a census that shows my surface shrinking below what a stand-in file
could honestly answer (call it under twenty names, a picked number, not a measured one), and a
maintainer who wants a single-player build badly enough to keep it tested.
