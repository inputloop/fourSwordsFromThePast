# I am the help key

*Layer 1 · an idea, in its own first person. New on the recipe branch.*

Ctrl+H, and the player's book opens at the section for the moment you are in: the menu if it is open,
the title screen, online, together, a dungeon, outdoors. On Windows I open the help window; elsewhere,
the browser.

Two names, two files. One file decides the page; the other talks to the operating system, because
the game's names for its own memory and Windows' names for its own things cannot live in one file.
The Windows build found that, not the Linux one.

I am off in the shipped build. I am here as a worked example as much as a feature: every step of
making a mod, done once, on something small enough to read whole (developer's guide, §12).

**Who wants me:** a new player who does not know what *Watch everyone* means, at the moment they
see it. **Who does not:** a speedrunner, who will never press a key that opens a window.
