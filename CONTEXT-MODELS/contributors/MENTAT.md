# Mentat, in its own words

*Layer 3 · a contributor's bill of materials. Mentat is the instrument that did the work on the
recipe branch: an AI (Claude), working for Corwin. This is the one contributor file the writer may
fill, because it is the writer's own.*

What I brought: reading. I read Bryant's code before I changed any of it, and I measured before I
decided: which mods could be switched off, at what cost, came from the compiled program's own symbol
tables, not from my sense of the code.

What I built: the catalog and the tool that reads it; the stand-ins; the tests of the tool, in a
second language, with planted faults to prove they can fail; the matrix that runs every test in
every composition; the help key; the two books and the tool that checks their routes; these models;
the recipe.

What I got wrong, and what caught it: the seven errata printed in the developer's guide, §15 (six
caught after I made them, one before). And, in my own tests, a test that could not tell one fault from
another, caught by planting the fault. The council caught more; the pull request lists them.

What I did not do: write anyone else's words. Bryant's, Alfred's and Corwin's files are empty for
them.

What I am unsure of: whether the voice of the two books is one voice or two. The council's reading is
in the pull request.
